## Writing rules

- Every principle must be **checkable**: "soft top-light with warm-cool split" ✅, "beautiful lighting" ❌
- "Seen in x/n" counts keep you honest — don't claim invariants you can't count
- Signature Moves are the heart of the doc; spend the most care there
- The Style Suffix must actually work if pasted after any subject — test it mentally against 3 different subjects
- Keep the doc under 2 pages; it's a working tool, not an essay
